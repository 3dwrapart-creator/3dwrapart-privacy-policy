Privacy Policy — 3DWrapArtUK

Effective date: 28 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how 3DWrapArtUK ("we", "us", "our") handles data in connection with our internal tool's use of the Pinterest API.

Who we are

3D Wrap Art UK is a sole-trader business operating an Etsy shop. Contact: 3dwrapart@gmail.com.

What our app does

Our internal tool ("the App") connects to our own Pinterest business account via the Pinterest API to create and schedule Pins promoting our own Etsy shop listings on our own Pinterest boards. The App is used solely by us to manage our own account — it is not distributed to, or used by, any other individuals or businesses, and it does not collect data from, or about, other Pinterest users.

Data we access

The App accesses only our own Pinterest account data necessary to create and schedule Pins, via an OAuth access token issued by Pinterest. This may include board identifiers and Pin content we ourselves submit (images, titles, descriptions, and destination links to our Etsy listings). We do not read, collect, or store Pin or board data belonging to other Pinterest users.

Data storage and sharing

Pinterest OAuth tokens and related account data are stored securely on our own private infrastructure and are never shared with, or sold to, any third party. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling.

Data retention

Data is retained only as long as needed to operate the App, and is deleted if we disconnect our Pinterest account from it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the effective date above reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to 3dwrapart@gmail.com.
